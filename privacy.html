Our Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to Cravefolio. We respect your privacy and believe visitors should understand how information may be handled when using our website.

This Privacy Policy describes our current website practices. Cravefolio is still in development, and certain planned features are not yet available.

1. Information We Collect

Cravefolio currently operates as an informational website and demonstration of a planned restaurant rewards platform.

We do not currently offer customer registration, restaurant account connections, or automatic rewards synchronization.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any other details you include.

Our website hosting provider may also process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, browser information, and security-related data.

2. How Information Is Used

Information received through direct communication may be used to:

  • Respond to questions or inquiries.
  • Review feedback and suggestions.
  • Improve Cravefolio and its planned features.
  • Address security or operational concerns.

We do not currently use visitor-submitted information to operate restaurant rewards connections.

3. Cookies and Website Analytics

Cravefolio does not currently operate its own advertising or personalized tracking system.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process technical information and use technologies necessary for website delivery, performance, and security.

If we introduce additional analytics, advertising services, or optional tracking technologies in the future, this policy will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

4. Third-Party Services

Cravefolio is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process information as part of delivering and protecting our website.

You can learn more about Cloudflare's privacy practices by visiting:

Cloudflare Privacy Policy

We do not currently provide live connections to third-party restaurant loyalty programs.

5. Restaurant Rewards and Future Features

Cravefolio aims to eventually provide a convenient way to view supported restaurant rewards in one place.

Any future account connections will depend on the availability of authorized integration methods and appropriate privacy safeguards.

Before introducing features that require additional personal information, we intend to update this policy to explain what data is collected, how it is used, and what choices customers have.

Please do not email us restaurant account passwords or other sensitive login credentials.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information submitted to us through direct email inquiries.

Information may be processed by service providers involved in website hosting and email delivery. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain email correspondence as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, maintain relevant records, and address legitimate operational or legal needs.

We aim to handle information responsibly. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have provided directly or to request its deletion.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable law and any relevant record-retention obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Cravefolio is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review the situation.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cravefolio develops or our practices change.

Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our information practices, please contact us.
