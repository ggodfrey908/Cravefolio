What Is Cravefolio?

Cravefolio is a restaurant rewards platform in development, created around one simple idea: keeping track of your favorite restaurant loyalty programs should be convenient.

Instead of switching between different restaurant apps to check your points, offers, and available rewards, our goal is to bring supported rewards information together in one organized dashboard.

Why We're Building It

Restaurant rewards are great, but remembering which app has your points, where you have an available offer, and when something expires can become frustrating.

We want Cravefolio to make it easier to see what rewards you have and enjoy the benefits you've earned.

How We Envision It Working

Our long-term goal is to make rewards tracking convenient without requiring customers to manually enter every reward.

1. Connect Link participating restaurant loyalty programs through authorized connections.
2. Discover View supported points, offers, and available rewards together in one place.
3. Enjoy Stay informed about your rewards and potential expiration dates.

These features are planned and will depend on the availability of approved restaurant integrations.

Where We Are Today

Cravefolio is currently in its early development stage. The rewards and restaurant information shown in our website preview is illustrative and does not represent connected customer accounts.

We are building the foundation first, with an emphasis on clarity, convenience, and responsible handling of customer information.
