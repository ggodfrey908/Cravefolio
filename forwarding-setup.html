Step 1 — Your private forwarding address

Keep this address private. Only forward restaurant reward messages you want Cravefolio to process. Some email formats and offers may not be recognized yet; check your dashboard after forwarding.

Step 2 — Verify your address with Gmail

  1. Open Gmail in a desktop browser.
  2. Go to Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  3. Choose Add a forwarding address and paste your private Cravefolio address.
  4. Confirm Gmail's prompts. Gmail should send a verification message to Cravefolio.
  5. Click Check for verification below. It may take a little time for the message to arrive.
  6. Use the confirmation code or link to complete Gmail's verification. Only proceed if you requested it yourself.

Step 3 — Create your combined Gmail filter

Instead of creating a separate forwarding rule for every restaurant, combine their verified sender addresses into one Gmail filter.

Sender directory preparation: Cravefolio's verified restaurant sender list is still being assembled. Until it is published, add only restaurant sender addresses you have confirmed from genuine emails in your inbox. Do not guess addresses.

Enter one email address per line. You can also separate addresses with commas. The generator removes duplicates.

Step 4 — Activate One-Time Sync

  1. In Gmail, click the search options icon beside the search bar.
  2. Paste your generated filter into the From field.
  3. Click Create filter.
  4. Select Forward it to.
  5. Choose your verified private Cravefolio forwarding address.
  6. Click Create filter to save.
Privacy reminder: Do not enable Gmail's general forwarding option for all incoming mail. Only forward messages matching your restaurant filter.

Cravefolio cannot independently confirm that you finished Gmail's filter setup. After activating it, send or receive a supported restaurant rewards email and check your dashboard. The filter applies to matching new incoming emails; it does not automatically import your entire email history.

Adding more restaurants later

When Cravefolio publishes additional verified restaurant senders, you can update your existing Gmail filter rather than creating another forwarding destination.

The combined filter must include each supported sender you want to forward. Newly added restaurants are not automatically included in an already-saved Gmail filter.
