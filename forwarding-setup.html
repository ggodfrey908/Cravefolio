Set up automatic rewards emails

Cravefolio gives your account a unique forwarding address. Forward restaurant rewards emails there to have supported offers added to your dashboard automatically.

Keep this address private. Only forward restaurant reward messages you want Cravefolio to process. Some email formats and offers may not be recognized yet; check your dashboard after forwarding.

Connect Gmail forwarding

  1. In Gmail on a desktop browser, open Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  2. Choose Add a forwarding address and paste your private Cravefolio address. Confirm Gmail's prompts.
  3. Gmail should send a confirmation email to Cravefolio. Use “Check for verification” below to see whether it arrived. It may take a little time.
  4. Use the confirmation code or link below to complete Gmail's verification. Only proceed if you requested this confirmation yourself.
  5. In Gmail, create a filter for the restaurant reward senders you choose. Select “Forward it to” and your verified Cravefolio address. Do not turn on forwarding for all incoming mail.
