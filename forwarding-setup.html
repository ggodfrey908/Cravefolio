4. Turn on your Gmail filter

  1. In Gmail, paste the copied filter into Search and review the results.
  2. Open Advanced search. Paste the filter into Has the words (leave From empty), then choose Create filter.
  3. Select Forward it to, choose your verified Cravefolio address, and save. Review any Gmail warning.
