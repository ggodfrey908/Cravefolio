Step 1 — Your private forwarding address

Keep this address private. Only forward restaurant reward messages you want Cravefolio to process. Some email formats and offers may not be recognized yet.

Step 2 — Verify your address with Gmail

  1. Open Gmail in a desktop browser.
  2. Go to Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  3. Choose Add a forwarding address and paste your private Cravefolio address.
  4. Confirm Gmail's prompts. Gmail should send a verification message to Cravefolio.
  5. Click Check for verification below.
  6. Use the confirmation code or link to complete Gmail's verification. Only proceed if you requested it yourself.

Step 4 — Activate One-Time Sync

  1. Click Copy Gmail Filter above.
  2. Open Gmail and paste the expression into Gmail's main search bar. Run the search and review the matching messages.
  3. Open Gmail's advanced search options.
  4. Paste the same complete expression into the Has the words field. Leave the From field empty.
  5. Click Create filter. Gmail may display a warning about search criteria; review it before continuing.
  6. Select Forward it to and choose your verified private Cravefolio address.
  7. Save the filter.
Privacy reminder: Do not enable general forwarding for all incoming Gmail messages. Only forward emails matching your restaurant filter.

Cravefolio cannot independently confirm whether you completed Gmail's filter setup. The filter applies to matching new incoming emails; it does not automatically import your entire email history.

Step 5 — Test your connection

After activating your filter, wait for a matching restaurant email or send a supported restaurant rewards message through your forwarding setup.

Then open My rewards to see whether Cravefolio successfully processed it.

Matching and forwarding an email does not guarantee its reward details can be extracted. Additional restaurant email formats will be supported as parsing is tested.
